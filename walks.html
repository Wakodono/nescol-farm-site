Walks

Explore the countryside from the farm. We have a signposted walk from the site—about 30–45 minutes on gentle paths and tracks. Great for day trippers and anyone wanting a short stretch of the legs before or after the cafe and shop.

Dogs on leads welcome on the walk. Sturdy shoes recommended; terrain can be uneven in places. See opening times for access.

Sunshine Farm Park, Between Edinburgh & Carlisle

Open Mon–Sun 9am–5pm
