Shop

Our farm shop stocks local produce and plants—seasonal fruit and vegetables, eggs, honey and preserves, plus a range of bedding plants and garden favourites. Perfect for gardeners and anyone looking for quality local goods.

Pop in after a walk or combine your visit with the cafe and petting zoo. We’re open Mon–Sun 9am–5pm.
